About this

Fixed-rate loan: payment = P x i / (1 - (1 + i)^-n), with i the monthly rate and n the number of months. The schedule rounds interest to cents each month. Checked against published figures, such as $200,000 at 6% over 30 years = $1,199.10 (example). Payment shown is principal and interest only: taxes, insurance and fees are not included. An estimate, not financial advice. Runs in your browser.
